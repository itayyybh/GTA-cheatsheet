The '''Clinton Residence''' (also known as the '''Forum Drive safehouse''') is the first [[Safehouses in GTA V|safehouse]] used by [[protagonist]] [[Franklin Clinton]] in {{V}}, and also appears in {{O}}.

==Description==
The safehouse is located on [[Forum Drive]], [[Strawberry]], and is unlocked after [[Franklin and Lamar]].

===Amenities===
The safehouse is a single-story bungalow where the player can save their game in Franklin's bedroom, which is located at the first door to the left of the front door. Across the hallway is a small living room with a watchable television and a supply of joints that Franklin can take a hit from. The living room leads on to a small kitchen, where Franklin can [[Drinking|drink]] a beer or use a [[Health|first aid kit]]. A door leading to the back garden can be found in the kitchen, but cannot be used. The interior of the house is characterized by its retro design and decorated with various spiritual and tribal ornaments and artwork. A garage can be found to the left of the property, which is large enough to store one car and one motorcycle (Franklin's [[Buffalo S]] and a [[Bagger]] obtained in an early mission spawn in the driveway). Following [[The Hotel Assassination]], if the player enters the house using cheats or in [[Director Mode]], Franklin's bedroom is cleared out (since he moved to [[Vinewood Hills]]). Chop's kennel can be found in the back garden. The [[Radio Stations in GTA V|radio station]] playing inside the house is [[Radio Los Santos]].

==Events of ''Grand Theft Auto V''==
At the beginning of the game, [[Franklin Clinton]] lives here with his aunt [[Denise Clinton|Denise]], after they inherited the house in Franklin's grandmother's will. A short time later, Franklin agrees to take care of [[Chop]], a [[Dogs|Rottweiler]] belonging to his friend [[Lamar Davis]], and Chop soon starts living at the house with Franklin and Denise.

Following Franklin's move to [[3671 Whispymound Drive]] in [[Vinewood Hills]] after the completion of [[The Hotel Assassination]], the house is turned into a "center for women" by [[Denise Clinton]] and both the house and garage are made inaccessible to the player ([[PlayStation 3|PS3]]/[[Xbox 360]]). In the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|enhanced]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V and Online: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S Editions|expanded & enhanced]] versions of the game, the garage remains accessible to the player, but the house still cannot be accessed. After Franklin moves out, Denise will send him an email barring him from re-entering the house. Once he has replied, Franklin tells her that the "only mistake" that his mother made was giving her the house and he tells Denise to stay away from him.

After Franklin moves to his new safehouse, he can sometimes be seen on Forum Drive driving away from the house, indicating that he was returning to retrieve some of his belongings or to make sure his aunt hadn't done anything drastic to the house. If he returns to the house, the front door will be locked and Denise will shout from inside, telling Franklin to go away. The two may exchange a few words.

==Events of ''Grand Theft Auto Online''==
The house plays no role in the storyline of ''Grand Theft Auto Online''. However, it is mentioned by Franklin and Lamar that Denise still resides in this house in [[2021]].

==Oversights==
*In the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|enhanced edition]], walking from the living room to the kitchen can pause whatever is on the TV. Returning to the living room or front door should fix it.

==Trivia==
*If the player fires a weapon at the house as [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] or [[Trevor Philips|Trevor]], [[Denise Clinton|Denise]] can be heard inside complaining and shouting. If the player continues, [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] will eventually call or text them to stop firing.
**Even after moving into [[3671 Whispymound Drive]] (and the following eviction of Franklin from the house), Franklin will still call Michael or Trevor if they shoot at the house.
*It is possible to access the interior of the safehouse after Franklin moves out, by going to the roof of the house, [[Switch Scenes|switching]] to another character and then switching back. The player spawns inside; however, they cannot save the game, even if playing as Franklin. This is not the case in the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|enhanced version]].
**Franklin can still interact with Denise; Michael cannot interact with her since they never [[canon]]ically met; Trevor also cannot interact with Denise despite [[Hood Safari|having previously met her]].
*For all characters, but more relevantly Franklin, if the player manages to get into the house after Franklin being evicted, they can still find Denise inside. Denise's presence or lack thereof is not synchronous with her annoyed exchange she has when Franklin "trespasses" and there will be no indication of the house's supposed conversion into a "women's centre" or even any other women besides Denise.
*A [[Bleeter]] post from Denise incorrectly states that the house is in [[Davis]].
*If the player enters the house (e.g. using mods) after Franklin no longer lives there, they will see that:
**Franklin's bedroom is now relatively bare.
**Franklin is still able to [[Drinking|drink]] beer in the kitchen and collect the two [[health]] pickups located in the house.
**All [[protagonist]]s are able to use the TV, as with any other safehouse.
*A picture in the house shows a young Franklin at [[BJ Smith Recreational Center]] wearing a [[Los Santos Panic]] top. This is likely an oversight because the [[Ballas (HD Universe)|Ballas]] support that team, whereas Franklin has always been associated with [[The Families]].
