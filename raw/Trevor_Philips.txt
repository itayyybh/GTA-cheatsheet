'''Trevor Philips''' is one of the three [[protagonist]]s (along with [[Michael De Santa]] and [[Franklin Clinton]]) of {{V}}, and a major character in {{O}}. 

Trevor is a career criminal and former bank robber with a troubled past and raging abandonment issues. After founding his own company, [[Trevor Philips Enterprises]], he seeks to expand his control over the illegal gun and drug trade in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. Upon learning of his former friend and partner-in-crime Michael's survival, Trevor, having presumed Michael to be dead for a decade, finds him and resumes their old partnership, but soon discovers troubling secrets about their shared past that lead him to question his loyalties.

Following the events of ''Grand Theft Auto V'', Trevor is referenced in content updates for ''Grand Theft Auto Online'' taking place after the single-player story. These confirm that Trevor has retired his company and left Blaine County for new pastures, but his current whereabouts remain unknown.

He is portrayed by [[Steven Ogg]].

==Background==
What is known about Trevor's background is highly speculative and comes from his own words. Trevor was born and raised in [[Canada]], near the border with the [[United States]], or, as he calls it, the "Canadian border region of America". He claims he "grew up in five states, two countries", in "fourteen different homes", "three care homes", "two correctional facilities", with "eight fathers", and "one beautiful, damaged flower of a [[Mrs. Philips|mother]]."{{Ref|name=TrevortoFranklin|ref=Trevor to Franklin during [[Friendships in GTA V|friend activities]]. {{Small|({{URL|https://youtu.be/Seg7NNVQCT8?t{{=}}9232|Video}})}}}}

As a child, Trevor had a history of rage issues and violent impulses that ruined any chances of fitting into society. He claims that, in a fit of rage, he sodomized his hockey coach with a [[Hockey Stick|hockey stick]]{{Ref|name=Sodom|ref={{Quote|He did for a while but his coach accidentally had a stick jammed up his ass.|Trevor to [[Wade Hebert]] in [[Friends Reunited]].}}}}, and at some point he supposedly strangled a clarinet player with his own instrument. Trevor also claims to have killed various [[animals]] and "drifters" even during the early years of his life.{{Ref|name=clarinet|ref={{Quote|Sounds like someone strangling a clarinet player. And I talk from experience.|Trevor to [[Cletus Ewing]] in [[Fair Game]].}}}}

Trevor's childhood itself was an unhappy one. His father was physically abusive towards him,{{Ref|name=father|ref={{Quote|My daddy was not nice to me!|Trevor to Debra in [[Hang Ten]].}}}} while his mother was emotionally abusive, overbearing, treated him with condescension and saw him as a "useless boy" who cared little about her. <!--While not confirmed, it is implied that, due the fact that Trevor hates to be called a "motherfucker" with this triggering two of his [[Rampages|rampage missions]] and what caused him to kill [[Johnny Klebitz]], his mother might have also been sexually abusive towards him as well.{{Fact|where?}}--> When he was a child, Trevor's father abandoned him in a shopping mall, which Trevor claims to have later burned down in retaliation. Trevor also had a brother named [[Ryan Philips|Ryan]], whom Trevor was not fond of. Ryan died in an "accident" prior to [[2013]].

Trevor dropped out of school, ultimately robbing him of some grammatical skills and basic knowledge, such as the location of ancient Rome, which he believed to be part of America. It is possible, however, that he had some formal education, as he claims{{Ref|name=education|ref=During a police chase in a [[Switch Scenes|switch scene]].}} — though possibly sarcastically — that he took a night class in criminal law. Despite this, he does have good mathematical skills, such as being able to accurately calculate the price of four tons of gold in a matter of seconds. Trevor also claims that he was the Canadian under-18 Golf Champion and "nearly went pro".{{Ref|name=pro|ref=During [[golf]].}} 

Later in his life, Trevor discovered that he had a talent for flying jets. He enlisted in the air force to fly fighter jets, but several days prior to completing his training and becoming a licensed pilot, Trevor was deemed mentally unstable during a psychological evaluation, thus resulting in his discharge and grounding for life. It is implied his discharge was due to his psychological relationship with his mother.

Trevor's first job involved robbing a place that cashed checks. However, he was caught because the clerk turned out to be someone who knew him, resulting in him serving four months of a six-month sentence. After Trevor was released, he and Michael committed many robberies all over the {{WP|Midwestern United States|Midwest}}, though Michael's recognizable quoting of lines from [[Solomon Richards]]'s movies, and Trevor's habit to snap and murder people in broad daylight often kept them on the run. Throughout their crime spree, they worked with several [[Moses|other]] accomplices; the closest one being [[Lester Crest]] {{nd}} a talented hacker and heist planner.

Over time, Michael developed a relationship with and married a stripper named [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]], eventually having two kids, [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]] and [[Jimmy De Santa|Jimmy]]. This created some friction between the criminals, due to Michael spending more and more time with his new family and less with his criminal activities. Despite this, Trevor began to see himself as an uncle to the couple's children, even vowing to avenge Tracey should anyone 'wrong her'. Over the following years, Michael became more cautious as a criminal because he saw himself having more to lose should he be imprisoned or killed, which led Trevor to believe Michael was going "soft". As a result, Trevor recruited a fellow crook named [[Brad Snider]] to take part in their robberies. Michael and Lester distrusted Brad, never showing any empathy towards him. At some point, Brad suggested to Trevor that they cut off Michael and work only with each other. Trevor refused, believing he should stay loyal to Michael.

[[File:Prologue-GTA5.jpg|thumb|280px|left|The last time '''Trevor''' would see Michael for nine years.]]

Trevor and Michael remained partners in crime until they undertook an [[Prologue (GTA V)|ill-fated heist in 2004]] alongside Brad and a [[Getaway Driver|getaway driver]] in [[Ludendorff]], North Yankton. Secretly, Michael made an agreement with corrupt [[Federal Investigation Bureau|FIB]] agent [[Dave Norton]] to exit his life of crime and start over with his family in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. The plan involved the group robbing a [[Bobcat Security Depot|cash depot]], followed by them making their way to a rendezvous point, where Dave would fatally shoot Trevor and non-fatally shoot Michael, with the latter then pretending to be buried at the [[Ludendorff Church and Cemetery|Ludendorff Cemetery]], before actually moving to [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] with his family.

The heist initially ran relatively smoothly until [[Alex (GTA V)|two]] [[Jaspers|security guards]] were killed {{nd}} the second one by Trevor as the guard held Michael at gunpoint {{nd}} and the [[North Yankton State Patrol|police]] arrived, which the group had to fight through, barely managing to get to their [[Rancher XL (snowed)|getaway car]]. As they drove to the rendezvous point, where they were told a helicopter was waiting for them, their driver was killed by the police and Michael was forced to take the wheel, though their vehicle was struck by an oncoming [[Freight Train|train]] at a railroad crossing and destroyed. Michael insisted that they stick to the plan, and they began walking in search of the helicopter. They were then ambushed by Dave, who fired at the trio, missing Trevor and fatally wounding Brad instead, before shooting again and "injuring" Michael. Trevor refused to leave his friends behind and attempted to fight off the police until a weary Michael told him to save himself, prompting Trevor to reluctantly make his escape.

As a result of the failed heist, both Trevor and Michael wrongly believed one another to be dead. Trevor was not able to get access to his savings because of his identity being blown, but regardless managed to evade the police for several years and made a new life for himself in the rural town of [[Sandy Shores]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. There, he developed an addiction to crystal [[Methamphetamine|meth]] and founded a small criminal business with new-found partners [[Ron Jakowski]], a paranoiac conspiracy theorist, and [[Chef]], a meth cook. The business, named [[Trevor Philips Enterprises]] (alternatively called Trevor Philips Industries), specialized in arms smuggling and the cooking and sale of crystal methamphetamine. During Trevor's time in Sandy Shores, he affiliated himself with the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (HD Universe)|Aztecas]] gang, the redneck hillbilly [[O'Neil Brothers]], and high-ranked members of [[The Lost MC]] {{nd}} including the club's leader, [[Johnny Klebitz]]. However, the latter's relationship was particularly strained due to Trevor's tendency to occasionally have sex with Johnny's girlfriend, [[Ashley Butler]], in exchange for meth payments, much to Johnny's chagrin.

Trevor, unaware that Brad had succumbed to his injuries, incorrectly believed he had been incarcerated as a result of the failed Ludendorff heist, and sent a letter to the federal prison system addressed to him. However, the letter was intercepted by the FIB, who established a correspondence to monitor him. The responses Trevor received from "Brad" were actually written by FIB trainees under the direction of Dave Norton.

In [[2012]], [[Fooliganz]] [[Wade Hebert]], [[Daisy Bell]], [[Kush-Chronic]], and several others stopped in Sandy Shores to buy drugs while on their way to "the gathering". They met Trevor, who took the group, save Wade, to a nearby [[Davis Quartz|quarry]] and is implied to have murdered them. Wade never figured out what happened to his friends and decided to stay in Sandy Shores, joining Trevor's criminal empire. At some point, Trevor rammed a hockey stick up Wade's anus, just like he had done before to his hockey coach.

Eventually, Trevor plans a heist and recruits the ''GTA Online'' Protagonist and several associates to [[Series A|take part]]. He tasks them with stealing drugs from several groups, such as the [[Los Santos Vagos (HD Universe)|Vagos]], The Lost MC, and the [[Ballas (HD Universe)|Ballas]], in order to sell them himself.

After the crew successfully takes the drugs, Trevor is at a warehouse, [[Series A Funding|ready to move the product]], but is informed that the gangs they robbed are coming back for their goods. After Trevor and the crew hold off the gangs, they rush to the [[El Gordo Lighthouse]], where the drug deal is set to take place. Trevor then gives the crew their money in advance and they leave. He stays at the lighthouse and waits for [[Phil (DOA Agent)|the buyer]] to arrive, but when he shows up, Trevor correctly determines that he is an undercover agent, and that the deal is a sting operation by the [[Drug Observation Agency]]. Trevor escapes by jumping off of a cliff into the ocean and swimming to safety. Later on, he angrily smashes his head against a tree before crying uncontrollably.

While trying to avoid a confrontation by simply walking away, he is pushed to his breaking-point as Johnny continues to express his anger. Trevor, no longer able to ignore Johnny's rage, mockingly invites him to have sex, and starts making jokes about his meth addiction, claiming that it is the reason for the friction between the two men. Johnny lays his guard down and tells Trevor that he still loves Ashley, to which Trevor responds by hugging and "consoling" him before he finally snaps. He suddenly grabs the biker by the neck and throws him to the ground, where he proceeds to smash his beer bottle against Johnny's face and stomp repeatedly on his head, killing Johnny as Wade, Ron, and Ashley watch in horror.

Knowing that Johnny's gang will be outraged over their leader's callous murder, Trevor decides to preemptively [[Mr. Philips|wipe out]] The Lost MC and their meth business from Blaine County before they have a chance to exact revenge. He drives with Wade and Ron to a Lost [[R L Hunter & Sons|hangout]] in [[Grapeseed]] where [[Terry Thorpe]], [[Clay Simons]], and several other members of the Lost are. While driving, he has the option to run over Ashley as she cries over Johnny's body. After announcing and mocking Johnny's death, the members of The Lost retreat to [[The Range|their main hideout]], to notify the rest of the gang. Trevor aggressively follows them there in his [[Bodhi|Canis Bodhi]], with Ron and Wade tagging along. As he chases the bikers, Trevor can optionally kill Terry and Clay by shooting or running them off their bikes.

When they arrive, Trevor, alongside Ron and Wade, attacks the hideout and wipes out a number of Lost members. Afterwards, he orders Wade to find out where Michael Townley lives. In the meantime, Trevor and Ron do more work, including attacking the trailer of [[Varrios Los Aztecas (HD Universe)|Aztecas]] leader [[Ortega]], along with optionally killing him, and [[Nervous Ron|taking over]] an [[Sandy Shores Airfield|airfield]] run by The Lost MC, after they vandalize Trevor's trailer as revenge for him killing Johnny.

[[File:CrystalMaze-GTA5.png|thumb|280px|left|'''Trevor''' preparing to assault the O'Neils.]]
Trevor attempts to do business with the [[Los Santos Triads]], led by a man known as [[Wei Cheng]], who wants a reliable partner to help expand his business operations in Blaine County. Wei sends his son, [[Tao Cheng]], along with a [[Tao Cheng's Translator|translator]] to meet with Trevor, who attempts to show them [[Ace Liquor|his meth lab]]. During the tour, he is forced to hide the translator and Tao in a freezer when members of the Aztecas attack, resulting in a [[Trevor Philips Industries|shootout]], which Trevor emerges victorious from. Ultimately, however, the Triads cancel the potential deal with Trevor and choose to do business with the rival [[O'Neil Brothers]] gang instead.

Enraged after learning this, Trevor decides to kill the O'Neils. On the way to their ranch, Trevor receives a phone call from [[Elwood O'Neil]] who invites Trevor to talk so they can smooth the issue over, but Trevor declines the offer and lets Elwood know what he plans to do. A panicked Elwood tells his brothers and associates to protect the farm before leaving with two of his other brothers, [[Walton O'Neil|Walton]] and [[Wynn O'Neil|Wynn]], to meet with the Triads. Despite the O'Neils' efforts, Trevor [[Crystal Maze|attacks]] the [[O'Neil Ranch]], slaughtering the brothers and obliterating their farm by lighting a gas trail he made using a [[Jerry Can]] from their basement meth lab.

A short while later, Trevor receives news from Wade that two people named Michael Townley live in Los Santos, but neither match Trevor's description, as one is an old man and the [[Michael Townley (kindergartner)|other]] is in kindergarten. However, Wade claims that a man who matches Michael Townley's profile named "Michael De Santa" lives in Los Santos, has two children and is married to a woman named Amanda. Trevor recognizes his old partner's alias and decides to take a [[Friends Reunited|trip]] to the city with Wade. Before leaving, he briefly visits The Lost MC's main hideout as a new chapter was recommissioned there to continue business. He destroys their camp entirely with [[Sticky Bombs]], further removing the group's presence from Sandy Shores. Afterwards, he arrives in the city and locates an [[Floyd's Apartment|apartment]] where Wade's cousin [[Floyd Hebert|Floyd]] lives, deciding to stay there, despite Floyd's protests.

[[File:TrevorPhilips-GTA5-Yoga.jpg|thumb|280px|right|'''Trevor''' surprising everyone with his sudden appearance.]]
Trevor then tracks down Michael to his [[De Santa Residence|home]] in the affluent district of [[Rockford Hills]], surprising and shocking everyone in the house, who all believed Michael's word that Trevor was likely dead. When Trevor asks where [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]] is, [[Jimmy De Santa|Jimmy]] reveals that she is auditioning for ''[[Fame or Shame]]''. Trevor and Michael rush to the [[Maze Bank Arena]], knowing well that Tracey will humiliate herself with her terrible dancing on TV. The two disrupt the audition, where Tracey is just as surprised as the rest of her family to see Trevor. When the presenter of the show, [[Lazlow Jones (HD Universe)|Lazlow Jones]], begins to make sexual advances towards Tracey during her dance, Trevor and Michael become angry and [[Fame or Shame (mission)|chase]] Lazlow to the [[Los Santos River (HD Universe)|Los Santos River]], where Trevor humiliates the reality TV star by making him take off his pants and dance while he films it on his mobile phone, before leaving.

After learning that Floyd works at the [[Port of Los Santos]], Trevor, along with Wade, forcibly accompanies him to his job, disguised as dock workers to [[Scouting the Port|see if anything worth stealing is present]]. While they are there, Trevor learns that a private security force named [[Merryweather Security|Merryweather]] has a [[SS Bulker|freighter ship]] containing secret cargo. Assuming the object is valuable, he plans a heist to steal the cargo for himself with the help of Michael, whom Trevor feels "owes him one".

Before the planned robbery can go underway, however, Trevor, along with Michael, are contacted by a highly-decorated yet highly-corrupt [[Federal Investigation Bureau|FIB agent]] named [[Steve Haines]] for assistance in [[Three's Company|extracting]] an [[Azerbaijan|Azerbaijani]] prisoner named [[Ferdinand Kerimov]], who is being held by the [[International Affairs Agency|IAA]] at their [[IAA Headquarters|headquarters]]. Before the operation is underway, Michael introduces Trevor to his recently antiquated friend, a Families gang member named [[Franklin Clinton]]. Trevor assists in the operation by flying the [[Frogger|helicopter]] used to allow Michael to extract the prisoner from the building, which he promptly keeps for himself afterwards 'in lieu of payment'.

[[File:TrevorPhilips-GTA5-SS1.jpg|thumb|280px|left|'''Trevor''' demanding to taste the other side of the brick.]]
Trevor later visits Franklin in [[Strawberry|his neighborhood]], telling him and his friend [[Lamar Davis|Lamar]] that he is looking to make "friends". He accompanies the pair on a minor drug deal at [[Grove Street (HD Universe)|Grove Street]] set up by a fellow gang member named [[Stretch (character)|Stretch]], much to Lamar's annoyance. The deal initially goes smoothly, until Trevor grows suspicious and attempts to take a sample of the [[cocaine]] brick they were going to purchase. He snaps the brick in half, revealing a key inside and that it was actually a piece of drywall. A [[Hood Safari|gunfight]] breaks out, and the three have to fight through a group of oncoming rival Ballas gang members. The [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD]] arrive, so Lamar leads Franklin and Trevor down into the Los Santos River. Hijacking [[Speedophile|jet skis]] from a [[MC Clip|rapper's]] professional photo shoot, the trio escape via the river from the oncoming police.

Trevor is brought back to work for the FIB once more, this time to [[By the Book|interrogate]] Kerimov, the man he helped to rescue from the IAA. Trevor extracts information out of Kerimov by torturing him, while Haines observes and asks the questions, before relaying the answers to Michael and Dave Norton, who are supposed to find and assassinate alleged Azerbaijani terrorist [[Tahir Javan]]. After the job is done, Haines orders Trevor to kill Kerimov and dispose of his body before leaving. Instead of following Haines' orders, Trevor helps Kerimov escape by driving him to the [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos airport]] and telling him to run away in order to become an advocate against government-sanctioned torture, despite the fact that Kerimov lives in Los Santos, has American citizenship, and has family living there as well.

Eventually, Trevor gets to put his [[The Merryweather Heist|heist]] into motion and manages to steal the target cargo with the help of Michael and Franklin. However, Michael and Trevor's old contact whom the former had recently been working with again, Lester Crest, arrives to discourage Trevor from taking the cargo, as it is in fact a superweapon that the U.S. government were planning to trade to the Chinese and will stop at nothing to recover it, especially if it is in the hands of a person like Trevor. Trevor initially refuses to give it up, but is forced to do so when Michael and Franklin agree it is not worth keeping due to its dangerous nature.

Following the heist, Trevor receives a call from [[Elwood O'Neil]], who threatens to kill Trevor for murdering his brothers and destroying the O'Neil family ranch in Sandy Shores. Trevor ignores the threat and invites Elwood and what remains of his family to come and find him in Los Santos before hanging up the call.

[[File:TrevorPhilips-GTA5-Paleto.jpg|thumb|280px|right|'''Trevor''', Michael and Chef in [[The Paleto Score]].]]
Getting over the failed heist, Trevor continues to do jobs with Michael and Franklin, including [[Blitz Play|stealing]] money from an [[Securicar|armored car]] and [[I Fought the Law...|helping]] [[Eye in the Sky|to]] procure luxury vehicles for a billionaire named [[Devin Weston]], the latter of which [[Pack Man|they are not paid for]], greatly enraging them. Eventually, Trevor takes a job with Michael that involves [[Caida Libre|assassinating]] the [[Javier Madrazo|cousin]] of [[Mexico|Mexican]] drug lord [[Martin Madrazo]], for planning to testify against him in a court case. Trevor is under the assumption that Michael brought him on to help case Madrazo's [[La Fuente Blanca|house]], but when he mentions this, Michael clarifies they are not going to rob Madrazo, annoying Trevor. After Michael shoots down the [[Shamal|plane]] that Madrazo's cousin was flying on, Trevor finds the crash site and kills the cousin, who survived the crash, then recovers the testimony. As Trevor drops it off at Madrazo's ranch, payment negotiations fall through and he witnesses Madrazo mistreating his wife, [[Patricia Madrazo|Patricia]]. Trevor, having developed an infatuation with her and already being in a poor mood over not getting to rob Madrazo, severs one of his ears and kidnaps Patricia, forcing himself and Michael to go into hiding at the former's trailer. During this time, the pair are unable to enter Los Santos without being attacked by Madrazo's men.

Whilst in hiding, Trevor continues [[Minor Turbulence|trying]] to run his business, [[Predator (mission)|kills]] what's left of the O'Neils with Michael and Franklin, and [[The Paleto Score|helps rob a bank]] in [[Paleto Bay]] in order to secure funding for [[Monkey Business|another FIB job]], which then requires him to help Michael and Dave Norton escape the [[Humane Labs and Research]] facility as they try to steal a neurotoxic chemical being developed there. Eventually, Michael repays Madrazo with [[Derailed|an artifact he and Trevor steal]] from a Merryweather train, while a reluctant Trevor returns Patricia to her husband, whom he threatens with further violence should he ever mistreat his wife again. With his problems with Madrazo now taken care of, Trevor and Michael are once again able to safely enter Los Santos.

Upon Trevor's return to Floyd's apartment, he encounters Floyd's girlfriend [[Debra]], who has returned home from a business trip overseas. Debra quickly becomes annoyed with Trevor's presence, demanding that he leave. Trevor drives Debra to her limits after suggesting that she caught crabs at the conference she attended and that they were "regular little fuck fests", and eventually breaks down crying and makes a mocking proposal to marry Debra and Floyd. An enraged Debra brandishes a gun at Trevor, and Floyd picks up a nearby knife after mustering some courage, to which Trevor tells them that they "are not very fucking nice". In the ensuing chaos, Floyd is shot in the head and Debra is stabbed to death. After leaving the apartment, a blood-stained Trevor encounters Wade, who asks where Floyd is. Trevor lies to him and says that Debra and Floyd mysteriously "[[Hang Ten|disappeared]]" and probably won't come back. Wade and Trevor then move into the [[Vanilla Unicorn]] strip club after "taking it over" by murdering the [[Leon (GTA V)|previous manager]]. There, he meets with Michael, Franklin, and Lester to plan the robbery of the [[Union Depository]]. In order to do so, they [[Surveying the Score|survey]] the depository for any possible escape routes. Michael and Franklin observe by car, while Trevor and Lester observe by helicopter.

[[File:TrevorPhilips-GTA5-SS2.jpg|thumb|280px|left|'''Trevor''' digging up Michael's grave to confirm his suspicions.]]
After meeting up with Michael at his mansion, Trevor tries to convince Michael that they bust Brad out of prison and start robbing banks like they used to do years ago. Michael refuses, however, due to the fact that he has a family and wants to make movies, which irritates Trevor. Because of Michael's reluctance to talk about Brad and Trevor's suspicions about what actually happened in North Yankton and who was really buried in Michael's grave, Trevor deduces that Michael betrayed him and flies back to North Yankton, with Michael in pursuit. Trevor arrives first at the graveyard and exhumes Michael Townley's grave. His suspicions are confirmed when he finds Brad's decaying corpse in Michael's place. After a stand-off at the grave, during which Michael somewhat explains the deal that took place between him and Dave Norton, Trevor, feeling hurt and betrayed by Michael's revelation, flees when the duo is [[Bury the Hatchet|ambushed]] by Wei Cheng's Triads, who had followed Michael from the airport, hoping that he would lead them to Trevor.

As Trevor flies back to San Andreas, he receives a phone call from Wei Cheng who threatens Trevor, claiming that he kidnapped Michael and that he will kill him if he does not surrender his business in Sandy Shores, mistaking Michael as Trevor's gay lover due to the fact that they lived together in his trailer for the period of time while Madrazo was after them. Trevor ignores the threat due to his newly ignited feud with Michael and tells Cheng to do as he pleases.

Much later, Trevor meets up with Franklin at his [[Clinton Residence|aunt's house]] in [[Strawberry]]. ===Appearance===
{{Quote|Thin, sallow, definitely an addict of some sort. Smells like he hasn't bathed in weeks. White, middle-aged.|[[Josh Bernstein]] in [[Breach of Contract]].}}
Trevor has an athletic build. He has brown eyes and receding black hair. In his official [[Grand Theft Auto V/Artwork|artwork]], however, he has dark brown hair.

He also has several cuts on his face along with several tattoos including one along his neck featuring a dotted line and the words "CUT HERE", and a memorial tattoo for Michael on his shoulder, as well as a flying bird behind his ear, blazing skull on his left hand and the words "FUCK YOU" across his knuckles. Trevor has a visible scar on his abdomen from what looks to be a [[knife]] wound. Trevor also has numerous sores or boils over his lower stomach and back, likely from his drug addictions.

Trevor's default outfit is a plain and dirty white V-neck, ripped up dirty blue jeans or sweatpants and a pair of brown boots. He is seen in one screenshot with a different outfit on—a jean jacket with a half buttoned up shirt along with a white undershirt and a pair of blue jeans. Also, in some screenshots, during the second {{V}} [[Grand Theft Auto V/Trailers|trailer]] and wearing his default outfit, he is seen wearing a calculator watch on his left wrist.

During the [[Prologue (GTA V)|Prologue]], which took place 9 years before the game's events, Trevor sports a different appearance. He isn't bald (although his hairline has started to retreat and there's a small bald spot on the back of his head), has a mullet covering his entire back of the neck, and sports a mustache. These facial features can be purchased at [[barbers]] around Los Santos, although Trevor will still be balding with the mullet, like all his hair options. He also lacks his "CUT HERE" tattoo.

He seems fond of buying cheap clothing from Binco and the Discount Shop, while he shows total disdain for buying clothes from Ponsonby's. Some characters also mention that he is an extremely untidy and messy person when it comes to clothes and hygiene. When Michael and Trevor [[Fame or Shame (mission)|reunite]], Michael claims Trevor "[stinks] like piss" and has blood under his fingernails. During the [[Paleto Score Setup]], Michael calls Trevor a "proto-hipster" because his clothes are never clean and never fit. Also, [[Lamar Davis]] claims that Trevor has a "smell of ass on the distance of three meters."

There is a running joke during the story of ''GTA V'' of characters mistaking Trevor for some celebrity or movie actor. During the [[Strangers and Freaks]] mission [[Nigel and Mrs. Thornhill]], the couple [[Nigel]] and [[Mrs. Thornhill]] mistake Trevor for being the actor and stuntman [[Jock Cranley (HD Universe)|Jock Cranley]]. After [[Franklin]] met Trevor in [[Three's Company]], he brings him up to [[Beverly Felton]] during [[Paparazzo - The Partnership]], where Beverly initially believes Trevor was on the show [[The Men's Room]], which Franklin dismisses. There is also unique pedestrian dialogue for when the player is using Trevor, where pedestrians may mention how Trevor looks exactly like someone they saw in [[Midget Fuckers 22]].

===Personality===
{{Quote|Out here, I can be mean, bullying, selfish. Do the things that make me, me!|Trevor Philips}}
{{Quote|He's [...] hell walking on earth!|Michael De Santa}}

Trevor has been described as a difficult person to deal with: extreme, impetuous, vengeful, sociopathic, unhinged, unpredictable, untamed, infamous, homicidal, and prone to violent outbursts and destructive rampages. He does everything in a sociopathic and relentless manner and, although he's like this, he's honest about it and will never show hypocrisy. He has his own charm along with his own principles. However, [[Dan Houser]] has confirmed that Trevor "may not be all he seems" and is still "too horrible" to be a hero of any kind. He is also quite insecure about being born in [[Canada]] and takes offense to people mocking his accent. Almost all of his [[Rampage (GTA V Missions)|Rampages]] are caused by Trevor flying into rage over being mocked over his Canadian accent. During a mission, Ron asks Trevor about his business dealings in Canada, resulting in Trevor getting defensive and demands for Ron to clarify. Ron hastily corrects himself by asking about Trevor's business dealings in North Yankton "along the Canadian border", defusing a possible violent outburst from Trevor, thus showing his insecurity for being Canadian. Also, while hanging out with Michael and [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]], the former will sometimes remark "You're not even American." during a conversation. Somewhat enraged, Trevor will reply "I am! Fuck you!" and Michael will respond with "Whatever you say, eh." mocking Trevor's accent. This further shows his insecurity about being Canadian.

Trevor's mere presence always puts others on edge due to his extremely short temper and unpredictable, impulsive behavior. Ron, who is one of Trevor's friends and an unlikely one at that, is constantly terrified of him and frequently tries to appease Trevor whenever he can, especially if he is close to getting angry. Michael is also wary of him when they reunite, stepping in front of [[Jimmy De Santa|his son]] to protect him. Despite Ron's fear and Trevor's sociopathic behavior, they do seem to show a level of trust to each other as Trevor usually tasks [[Ron Jakowski|Ron]] to do a few missions for him such as blowing up [[The Lost MC]] fuel tank and helping him drop off cargo via a plane, as well as running [[Trevor Philips Enterprises]] in his absence. Wade, another friend, is equally scared of Trevor. Though Trevor torments and punches Wade often, this bullying extends to [[Wade Hebert|Wade]]'s cousin, [[Floyd Hebert|Floyd]], as well. Even strangers who have seen very little of him can be put off by his presence, shown when he instantly put [[Fabien LaRouche|Fabien]] on edge despite not even knowing him in the opening cutscene for the mission [[Fame or Shame (mission)|Fame or Shame]]. In [[The Merryweather Heist]], he gets angry quickly when his score is compromised. In the offshore ending, he gets so angry that he rams his face onto a wooden board and repeatedly smashes his face on it. His personality is stated to be partly based on that of legendary English prisoner {{WP|Charles Salvador}}, also known as "the most violent prisoner in Britain". He is a man known for his sudden violent outbursts which have earned him fame.

Out the three protagonists, Trevor can be said to be a true sociopath, as he easily kills with no remorse, has the typical parental issues (abandoned by his father and sensitive about his mother), is a pyromaniac, frequently abducts people and takes them to the [[Altruist Cult]] (the cult in question is implied to be made up of cannibalistic killers of which Trevor seems to be aware of) which he calls his "friends in the mountains" and has the habit of making sadistic jokes about his victims. For example, Trevor makes a joke about the piece of [[Johnny Klebitz]]'s brain left under his boot after he killed him, and telling [[Elwood O'Neil]] over the phone that Elwood had "too many [[O'Neil Brothers|brothers]]" and that they needed "culling". When Trevor kills Johnny, he demands that Johnny get up to face him, apparently unaware that he killed him. It is extremely unlikely that he cared though due to his plans to get rid of The Lost and how enraged he was with Johnny. In contrast with this behavior, however, is his protectiveness of Michael's family and his apparent grief when he thought Michael was dead, which shows a level of emotion not common in most sociopaths as they usually have little to no positive emotions for other people. In addition to this he refused to leave Michael in the prologue, while he was on the ground after being shot. Trevor seems to only care about the people very close to him and he can be truly loyal to them, which specifically includes his mother, Patricia Madrazo, [[Maude Eccles|Maude]], Michael, and later on even Franklin and Lamar. Brad's death affected him harshly, leaving him to blame Michael for what happened nine years ago back in North Yankton.

In contrast with his heavily sociopathic behavior, he has shown many times how needy he is for love and care. He tells Michael repeatedly how much he mourned him, to the point that he got a memorial tattoo with Michael's name on it. As he was being confronted by Floyd and Debra, he told them how much he wanted to be with them. His relationship with Patricia was also a display of true love and, in his words, he enjoyed a lot of the time spent with her.

Despite his weak relationship with [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]] and having a strained friendship with Michael, Trevor does care for Jimmy and [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]], viewing them as surrogate niece and nephew. Evidence of his caring is chasing [[Lazlow Jones (HD Universe)|Lazlow Jones]] when he does a sexual pose against Tracey as she performs a stripper-like dance and forcing him to take off his pants and dance in his underwear. Also, he will not allow Jimmy to drink alcohol when hanging out with him. Despite this, Trevor will insult Jimmy and Tracey when he encounters them outside of missions.

Trevor also seems to sometimes dwell on respect. When Wade calls [[Ashley Butler]] a "bitch", Trevor asks him if he thinks it's clever to disrespect women. He also often tells some of his friends to show some respect to himself, and sometimes even others. In the mission [[Trevor Philips Industries]], despite [[Chef]] worrying about the arrival of the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (HD Universe)|Aztecas]] at the meth lab, Trevor still orders him to show manners to [[Tao Cheng]] and [[Tao Cheng's Translator|his translator]] who want to see the meth lab operation, calling them as their "guests".  When Wade is covered in sewage, Wade's cousin suggests calling the heist off, but Trevor responds by telling him to respect Wade for what he went through and so he should carry on with the heist.

Despite his behavior, Trevor possesses an awareness for guilt. This is seen in the [[Strangers and Freaks]] mission [[Minute Man Blues]] in which [[Manuel]], one of the innocent men captured by Trevor and the [[Civil Border Patrol]] members [[Joe (GTA V)|Joe]] and [[Josef]] on suspicion of being an illegal immigrant in the previous mission [[An American Welcome]], stands up to Trevor fearlessly despite the latter pointing a gun at him, and claims he and his family have migrated to America since more than 200 years ago, saying that he is more American than Trevor is. He criticizes Trevor for disregarding civil rights with "Trevor's friends", who are harassing innocent legal immigrants and, in Trevor's case, in return for money. He adds that, unlike the Civil Border Patrol, he will not give Trevor a single cent for helping him to stop the Civil Border Patrol. Fueled by this and Trevor having already felt the Civil Border Patrol's actions were indeed illegal, he storms into a rage and hunts down and puts an end to the Civil Border Patrol once and for all. Also, when Trevor runs over a main character, he will often scream "No!" in panic, although he may instead blame the person for being in his way in an unconcerned way.

Trevor uses threats of sexual violence to show his dominance over other men, as well as enjoying making them uncomfortable. Many of his sarcastic remarks have been misinterpreted as sincere statements by players. He seems to take it a step further with [[Floyd Hebert|Floyd]], as there are many things that suggest a sexual relationship. Notably, if the player switches to Trevor during his stay at Floyd’s condo, sometimes Trevor can be seen looking over Floyd as he lays in the fetal position wearing women's clothing, uttering an apology to [[Debra]] while sobbing. However, this could just be Trevor showing his dominance by humiliating Floyd. Also, if the player chooses to catch [[Ralph Ostrowski]], Trevor implies that he sexually abused men while in prison. It should be noted that this behavior is only present when in the presence of weak people such as Ron, Wade, Floyd, and a meth-addicted Johnny Klebitz.

On the other hand, he respects and encourages bravery on his friends and partners in crime. During job preparations, he often tends to taunt and call out anyone who shows a sign of doubt, fear or weakness, and when they get back angrily at him, Trevor applauds and approves their reaction as a way to man them up.

In direct contrast to his erratic and chaotic nature, Trevor also appears to be highly intelligent and somewhat cultured. Although seemingly lacking of refined tastes, Trevor speaks in the most eloquent manner out of all three protagonists, utilizing elaborate and complex vocabulary as well as displaying a surprisingly dry wit. It must be noted that having studied 4 years at a military academy, Trevor is the only ''GTA'' protagonist to date with a college degree, possibly explaining his use of complex vocabulary and hinting at a sophisticated nature that he might or might not be intentionally masking. Trevor is incredibly focused and professional on the jobs at hand, displaying a surprising capacity for calculation and planning, even under duress and in highly chaotic situations. He can follow the plans strictly and also he has proven to have great ideas for big jobs and lead them successfully, These include [[Series A|stealing the drug stashes belonging to many dangerous groups]] and [[The Merryweather Heist|stealing a multi-million secret weapon]] from a [[Merryweather Security|heavily armed security group]]. However, these operations were failures since they lacked a prior careful inspection and were performed hastily (one being a police sting, the other putting the crew in extreme danger worldwide).

Trevor has shown some hints that he feels genuine sexual attraction towards men too, besides his normal sexually dominant behavior towards weaker men. He showed certain interests in the "stag do running man" [[Random Events in GTA V|random encounter]]. While picking up a couple of hitchhikers, the very inebriated couple begins to have sex as Trevor drives the vehicle. He offers to join in and perform fellatio on the boyfriend. In the mission [[I Fought the Law...]], Trevor touches the private parts of one of the guys as he's dressed as a policeman. During a drinking outing with [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]], he admits he has previously performed fellatio on men ("I'd rather suck a dick than smoke weed... and trust me, I've done both!"). In another conversation during an outing with [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] and Franklin at the same time, he admits he's bisexual after Franklin asked him whether he's gay or not.

Trevor is shown to be disgusted by racism, misogyny, as well as self-righteous and hypocritical attitudes which he considers "fake". He criticizes the Minute Men and the [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD]] for their racist behavior, reprimands Wade for his remarks toward Ashley, threatens Martin for mistreating Patricia, and is clearly disturbed by Josh Bernstein allowing Trevor to have sex with his wife (he claims he would not have done so if he knew, but then proceeds to take back this comment). After torturing Ferdinand Kerimov, he mocks the FIB's use of torture as a means of interrogation, claiming it is only a method of showing dominance and that the government fails to admit this. In one of his character switch scenes, he can even be throwing a Lost MC member off a bridge for being a bigot.

While it is never stated in-game, Trevor shows textbook signs of {{WP|Intermittent explosive disorder}}: a mental condition which includes episodes of impulsive behavior that could result in serious damage to anyone and anything around them, aggression grossly disproportionate to what provoked it, and episodic violence that cannot be categorized with any other existing mental and physical medical condition.

Trevor also appears to suffer from {{WP|coulrophobia}}, since in [[Grass Roots - Trevor]], dialogue suggests that Trevor once was or still is scared of clowns, saying such things as "I'm a big boy now" and "you can't hurt me anymore" (paraphrasing). He might also ask the clowns, "Are there any more sex offenders out there!?" This implies that, as a child, Trevor was sexually assaulted by a clown, thus causing his fear of clowns. Additionally, when the player gets Lamar, Franklin, and Trevor to hang out together off-mission, Franklin will mention he's sitting next to "Two Clowns" (Trevor and Lamar). Trevor then starts to get a little worried, asking, "Clown? Where?!" which may prove he is still scared of clowns.

{{Dialogue|p1=Michael|d1=The fuck is ''wrong'' with you?|p2=Trevor|d2=Oh, I got abandonment issues. I see a shrink once a week.|quotedetail=Michael and Trevor in [[By the Book]].}}

Although very rarely seen in-game, Trevor appears to be secretly ashamed and a bit scared of his personality. When hanging out with both Michael and Franklin simultaneously, Trevor might reveal in an ashamed tone that he doesn't want his mother to see him like this. If Trevor hangs out with Lamar enough times, he will admit to Lamar that sometimes he realizes how he's thinking (fixation on violence, horrible brutalities etc.) and gets scared of himself. If Trevor huffs gas on his trailer porch, he may sometimes mutter that he feels suicidal. This could hint that all the reckless stunts Trevor commits (such as flying a [[Duster]] inside a cargo plane while getting shot at with an [[RPG]]) might be subconscious attempts of getting killed. When drunk with Franklin, Trevor talks about being lonely and asks Franklin to hold him. When Franklin replies with jokes, Trevor starts crying, "Why are you joking!? I said I'm lonely!"

While Trevor is often controlling, loud, and willing to hurt anyone who he perceives as a threat in some way, whether physically or mentally, he is terrified by his mother. Upon meeting her in his trailer in the mission [[Mrs. Philips (mission)|Mrs. Philips]], he speaks incoherent sentences, is unable to answer questions and runs off to do the job she assigns to him without questioning it.

Trevor is also extremely talented at mathematics. According to Wade, Trevor is "really good with numbers" and can solve even the most difficult of math problems in his head in a matter of seconds without much (if any) hesitation, as seen in the Depository heist.

===Relationships===
{{Quote|Bring me my coffee or I'm gonna cut your arm off!|Trevor to Ron Jakowski in his own trailer.}}

'''[[Michael De Santa]]''' - Trevor's relationship with Michael started around the late 1980s, over twenty years prior to the game's events, when he and Trevor were escorting cargo across the border. Trevor claims that Michael's victim came after him, so Trevor shot a [[Flare Gun|flare]] into the other man's head and dumped the body using his plane (which Trevor claimed "Never smelled the same again."), and as soon as they left the plane, they both threw up and became friends. Michael roped Trevor into his many bank robberies and the two worked well together, growing a fondness for one another. At some point during their long partnership, however, there was a rift in their friendship, likely caused by Michael starting a family and wishing to settle down. Trevor began seeing Michael as "soft", but remained loyal to him and refused to abandon him even when Brad suggested that they cut him off. Unbeknownst to Trevor, Michael did not share his levels of loyalty, seeing Trevor as a reckless and dangerous psychopath. This eventually led him to make a secret deal with FIB agent Dave Norton to sell out his crew during their heist in North Yankton, after which both Trevor and Michael assumed one another to be dead. Trevor would subsequently mourn for Michael and at some point had "R.I.P Michael" tattooed on his arm to commemorate his best friend.

After their eventual reunion nine years later, Trevor is initially furious with Michael for not contacting him at any point after starting his new life in Los Santos, but quickly forgives him and the two resume their old friendship and partnership. Trevor seems to share a strange bond with Michael, as indicated by the fact that he tolerates his betrayal and occasional references to Trevor's Canadian roots, whereas others have been killed for far less. Once Trevor figures out what truly went down in North Yankton, however, he contemplates killing Michael before abandoning him to the mercy of [[Wei Cheng]]'s [[Los Santos Triads|henchmen]], later refusing to rescue him even when Wei Cheng contacts Trevor to tell him that Michael will be killed. After Franklin rescues Michael, Trevor, despite still hating the latter, comes to his aid during the shootout at the [[Kortz Center]], believing he needs Michael alive for their final score: the Union Depository heist. After the heist is pulled off successfully, Trevor is so pleased that he no longer wants to kill Michael, though he still sees him as a "snake".

In [[Something Sensible]], after Michael arrives to help Franklin kill Trevor, the latter states, ''I thought I was with one Judas! I'm surrounded by them.'' He then allows the pair to kill him, being so hurt by the betrayal of his closest friends that he has lost his will to live. In [[The Time's Come]], Franklin calls Trevor to help him kill Michael, but he refuses, saying he has enough "traitors" in his life. If Franklin meets with Trevor afterwards, the latter deduces that Franklin went through with killing Michael and calls him a ''Judas'' and a ''backstabber'' before warning him to stay away. It is implied Trevor was willing to forgive Michael, but never got the chance to apologize. In [[The Third Way]], Trevor and Michael continue to be at odds and aim their guns at each other until Franklin calms them down and explains that they need to work together to eliminate their enemies. Trevor is the first to relent and lower his gun, after which he, Michael and Franklin take out their various foes. In the aftermath, Trevor and Michael finally manage to rekindle their friendship, with Trevor considering Michael a friend, albeit one that he hates, which Michael accepts. If Trevor and Michael hang out afterwards, the latter extends a genuine apology to Trevor and offers him his share of the Big Score; Trevor declines the money but accepts the apology. On a subsequent hangout, Trevor will also apologize to Michael, claiming he shouldn't have acted the way he did. If Franklin hangs out with Trevor, he will ask him why he did not kill Michael, to which Trevor admits that he somewhat understands why Michael did what he did and that it would be wrong to kill him since he has a family. He also jokingly states that he and Michael both have "custody" over Franklin.

'''[[Franklin Clinton]]''' - Trevor is introduced to Franklin by Michael. They seem to be very good partners during the jobs they perform together, and later become close friends. Franklin is one of the few people who doesn't feel intimidated by Trevor's rage and violent outbursts. In fact, he usually manages to cool Trevor down in a collected manner. In the optional ending that involves [[Something Sensible|killing]] Trevor, he protests that he's been nothing but straight and true with Franklin. In the ending where Franklin [[The Time's Come|kills]] Michael, Trevor would cut off ties with him and claim that he's dead to him. Should all three protagonists [[The Third Way|survive]], Trevor will continue to see Franklin as a friend and tell him that he's "proud" of him when they hang out.

'''[[Ron Jakowski]]''' - Ron is the closest thing Trevor has to a "friend", besides Franklin and Michael, though their relationship is very one-sided. Trevor frequently abuses Ron both physically and verbally for his own amusement, while Ron only hangs around him out of fear, being very paranoid or outright terrified when spending time with Trevor. Despite Ron often being on the end of Trevor's temper, Trevor places high levels of trust in him, such as allowing him to run TP Enterprises in his absence. When introducing Ron to Michael, Trevor describes him as a "good guy, very loyal," and after Ron sources getaway vehicles for the duo in [[Derailed]], Trevor describes him as "dependable".

'''[[Wade Hebert]]''' - Wade appears to be good "friends" with Trevor and has been since they met the year before the events of the game. Wade is extremely loyal to Trevor and has no problem following his directions. Wade's stupidity and child-like personality cause him to occasionally screw up his tasks, which understandably gets on Trevor's nerves. In spite of Trevor being annoyed by Wade, he does generally care about him since he is useful for his business plans. Trevor also sees potential in Wade, as evidenced by the time when they met and Trevor caused Wade's friends Kush-Chronic and Daisy Bell to "disappear", while sparing Wade himself.

'''[[Floyd Hebert]]''' - Floyd appears to be terrified of Trevor. He's forced by Trevor to help him in his crimes, under menace of having his home and furniture trashed. In the mission [[Hang Ten]], he finally decides to stand up to Trevor, along with his girlfriend [[Debra]], ordering him to leave their condo. After a heated discussion, Debra pulls a gun on Trevor, and Floyd draws a knife to defend himself, making Trevor presumably kill both of them off-screen. Despite this, deep down Trevor may have actually cared for Floyd's miserable life, as in the mission [[Scouting the Port]] he spoke to Wade about bringing Floyd up from his low self-esteem. Later, he got out his penis to rest Floyd's insecurity about his own (though maybe for different reasons than care). He also notably had a concerned facial expression when Debra called Floy "crap" shortly before their murder.

'''[[Mrs. Philips]]''' - Trevor's mother is only seen during the [[Strangers and Freaks]] mission [[Mrs. Philips (mission)|Mrs. Philips]]. She is implied to have been an abusive and neglectful parent, while at the same time having very high expectations of her son and constantly chastising him. Before the events of the game, she was a stripper/prostitute, as seen in her and Trevor's comments (he states this sometimes when having a private dance). She was also an inmate and became disappointed in Trevor because he never wrote or visited her. During her mission, she asks if Trevor has found a woman yet and does not let him answer the question. She also asks if he's gay, because she has no problem with it. After the mission, she disappears and Trevor is seen crying on the floor. She is highly respected by Trevor, and he gets insulted should anyone ever bring her up, even if they are speaking positively of her. She is the only known person Trevor is afraid of.

'''[[Patricia Madrazo]]''' - Trevor first met Patricia when visiting her [[Martin Madrazo|husband's]] house alongside Michael, and immediately developed an attraction towards her. After Martin refuses to pay him for his work, Trevor, seeing how neglected and abused Patricia is by her husband, decides to kidnap her and hold her hostage at his trailer in [[Sandy Shores]]. As time goes on, Patricia is no longer tied up and is seen cooking meals for Trevor, which leads Michael to suggest both have contracted Stockholm syndrome. Trevor soon becomes protective of Patricia, and admits that he loves her and vice versa. When he is forced to return her to Martin, he does so tearfully and promises to rip off Martin's other ear if Trevor finds out he has been mistreating Patricia yet again. For the rest of the game, Patricia will secretly call Trevor on occasion. During the events of [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] in 2020, their relationship is confirmed to have been intimate, as they took pictures during their intercourse.

'''[[Lamar Davis]]''' - Lamar first met Trevor during the mission [[Hood Safari]]. Though Lamar initially felt uneasy in Trevor's presence and repeatedly asked Franklin who he was, he soon grew to like him, nicknaming him "Crazy White Dude" due to Trevor's behavior, and even telling Franklin to learn from his being "cold". Lamar will also warmly greet Trevor when meeting him during [[Lamar Down]].

'''[[Steve Haines]]''' - Trevor first meets Haines during a job to extract [[Ferdinand Kerimov]] from the IAA headquarters, and takes an immediate dislike of him. Haines forces him, Michael and Franklin into his operations, with Trevor in particular having to torture Kerimov to extract information that can be used to identify and kill [[Tahir Javan]]. Haines also gets the protagonists to perform several heists for him. However, more and more suspicion from the [[Federal Investigation Bureau|FIB]] falls on Haines, causing him to become extremely paranoid. He, along with [[Andreas Sanchez]], confronts Michael and [[Dave Norton]] at the [[Kortz Center]] and attempts to have them framed and arrested for ruining his career. After an ambush by the IAA, Merryweather and rival FIB forces, Haines kills Sanchez and escapes, leaving Michael and Norton to die; however, they manage to shoot their way out, with the assistance of Trevor, who wants Michael alive for the "Big Score". After the shootout, an infuriated Haines wishes to have Trevor, Michael and Franklin killed; Norton convinces him to have only Trevor killed as Franklin and Michael are easier to control. Haines then tasks Franklin with killing Trevor. If "Deathwish" is chosen, the protagonists team up and take down Haines' corrupt FIB unit and Devin Weston's private Merryweather battalion at a foundry, after which Trevor volunteers to deal with Haines, claiming he wanted to kill him from the moment they met. Using information provided by [[Lester Crest]], Trevor finds Haines shooting an episode [[The Underbelly Of Paradise|of his show]] on a ferris wheel at [[Del Perro Pier]], and kills him.

'''[[Devin Weston]]''' - Trevor meets Weston for the first time during a job for Steve Haines. Weston later offers Michael an opportunity to produce a film with Michael's idol - movie producer [[Solomon Richards]] - and also offers Franklin and Trevor a deal to steal five rare cars, which Weston will sell to government officials in China. However, he ultimately cheats them out of their payment, and also attempts to have Michael's family [[Meltdown (GTA V)|killed]] after Michael accidentally caused the death of Devin's [[Molly Schultz|lawyer]]. This incident leads Weston to order Franklin to kill Michael. If "Deathwish" is chosen, [[The Third Way|Trevor, Michael and Franklin take out Haines' corrupt FIB unit and Weston's Merryweather battalion at a foundry, after which they kill Haines, Stretch and Wei Cheng]]. After killing Haines, Trevor kidnaps Devin from his [[Devin Weston's Mansion|mansion]], shoves him inside the trunk of one of his cars, and drives him to a cliff, located at the foothills of [[Mount Chiliad]]. Michael and Franklin later show up and the three protagonists taunt a beaten Devin before pushing the car (with him still inside the trunk) off the cliff. The car repeatedly crashes into several rocks along the way, until it finally hits the [[Pacific Ocean (HD Universe)|ocean]] ground and explodes, killing Weston.

'''{{GTAOP}}''' - The ''GTA Online'' Protagonist first fell under Trevor's radar when they unwittingly stole an RV used by Trevor as a meth lab for [[Gerald]]. Upon realizing he cannot intimidate them, Trevor forces the Protagonist to carry out several jobs for him in order to pay back the debt he feels they owe him, eventually retaining their services in order to [[Series A|set up a drug deal]]. Trevor's relationship with the ''GTA Online'' Protagonist is surprisingly civil as he likely sees them as someone who is similar to him: a lost soul willing to commit whatever crime, no matter how dirty, as long as they're adequately paid for their services.

===Skills and Attributes===
Because he is a former military aviation pilot, he is skilled in both combat and piloting aircraft like helicopters. In the mission [[Three's Company]], Trevor was the one that flew the team to the skyscraper they needed to get their target out of. In the aftermath, as the team was pursued by law enforcement helicopters, he proved to be an able pilot under pressure.

Trevor is also the master of greed, ambition, and insanity (along with Michael and Franklin), and is seen in many ''GTA V'' screenshots performing many challenging (and some illegal) stunts. Some of those stunts include base jumping, jet-skiing, murdering, property destruction, arson, jumping out from moving vehicles and getting chased by the cops.

Wade also said that Trevor is "good with numbers", implying that he has good mathematical skills. This is confirmed during small talk with a bank executive during the Subtle approach to the final heist; upon being told the number of gold bricks they are stealing and the size of each one, Trevor rattles off the exact amount of gold they have before the executive can finish entering the above information into his calculator. Trevor also has an excellent perception and intuition, being the only one to figure out that the brick of coke Franklin and Lamar were buying from the Ballas at Grove Street was actually drywall.

He also proved to be a good strategist and planner. He alone pulled a successful heist on Merryweather Security Consulting, and he's been able to keep his relatively small organization peaking up in the drug and arms dealing against other bigger and more dangerous groups like the Aztecas, The Lost or the Marabunta Grande.

He also proved to be a powerful hand-to-hand combatant, usually knocking down people with a single punch in [[Fresh Meat (mission)|several]] [[Exercising Demons - Trevor|cutscenes]]. He's strong enough to lift and throw people off ledges in several of his [[Switch Scenes|switch scenes]].

Trevor's [[Special Ability|special ability]] is ''Invulnerability''. While active, Trevor can take an excessive amount of damage without dying as well as a special melee attack that he can perform. By enhancing this skill, the player can increase its maximum storage capacity, which directly affects its duration. Trevor's Special skill lasts for 30 seconds when the Stat Bar for Special skill has been maxed out. While in the ability, he can survive hits from trains, [[Rhino (HD Universe)|Rhino]] tank cannon shots and being mauled by a [[Cougars|mountain lion]].

==Reception==
Trevor has had very positive responses from many fans of ''GTA V'' and professional video game critics about his "fuck off!" personality, and extreme behavior. Trevor's grand appearance during the GI demo is considered an unforgettable one and sets up the character nicely, (the camera is zoomed in on Trevor's face but then zooms out to show him sitting on the toilet fighting constipation while yelling the famous quote, "''I need a fucking midwife for this thing!''").

===Dan Houser's Description of Trevor===
"''Trevor appeared to us pretty much out of nowhere as the embodiment of another side of criminality – of freedom, and of doing what you want. If Michael was meant to be the idea of some version of criminal control – or some sort of bourgeois criminal who tries to go straight and gets sucked back in – what about the guy who didn't do that? What about the opposite guy? What about the guy who just says ‘fuck off’ every moment, is relentless, doesn't want to stop taking drugs, doesn't want to stop partying, doesn't want to be told 'no' by anyone, and just completely revels in chaos?''"

"''Trevor was the other side of the GTA coin, I suppose. He's the person who's driven purely by desire, resentment, no thought for tomorrow whatsoever, completely id rather than ego-driven. Constant partying, constant madness. The only thing he doesn't want to do is stop. He wants to keep going and ride it all to the end. Won't take an insult from anybody. Kills without remorse, like a true psychopath, but very sentimental for the right reasons when it suits him. That seemed like another side of the kind of GTA coin or the GTA world, but very rarely as the protagonist. That seemed an interesting guy to have as a protagonist. Very unlike anyone we've had in a game before. If you began to develop him and Michael and their previous relationships... We wanted this feeling where you start off thinking one's good and the other's awful. Then you get spun the other way. Then you get spun back the first way, until you can't decide.''"

===General===
*Trevor's character is somewhat similar to that of {{WP|Paulie Gualtieri}} from ''{{WP|The Sopranos}}''.
**Trevor shows loyalty to Michael, who is similar to {{WP|Tony Soprano}}. Michael's kids also call Trevor 'uncle', much like Tony's kids do Paulie.
**Trevor's 'Grown Out' hairstyle resembles that of Paulie.
**Both have uneasy relationships with their mothers.
**Both are seen sniffing women's underwear.
**Both characters are referred to as being "psycho" and other similar terms.
**Both get angry when their families' native countries are insulted (Trevor is Canadian and Paulie is [[Italy|Italian]]).
**Trevor owns a strip club and used it as a base for his criminal operations, similar to how the DiMeo crime family used the {{WP|Bada Bing!}} strip club.
*Trevor's dedication to his mother and the fact that he can wear dresses may be a reference to {{WP|Norman Bates}} from {{WP|Alfred Hitchcock}}'s 1960 horror film ''{{WP|Psycho (1960 film)|Psycho}}''.
*In the [[Rush Casting Call]], Trevor's character name was labelled as "Simon". Interestingly, Steven Ogg also portrayed a character named {{WC|walkingdead|Simon (TV Universe)|Simon}} in ''{{WC|walkingdead|The Walking Dead (TV Series)|The Walking Dead}}''.
*Trevor's personality is likely based on the character Waingro from the movie ''{{WP|Heat (1995 film)|Heat}}''.

===''Grand Theft Auto V''===
*Trevor's initial backstory was that he grew up in the rural American backwoods. However, when [[Canada|Canadian]] actor [[Steven Ogg]] was hired to voice the character, the producers decided to work Ogg's thick Canadian accent into the character. As a result, Trevor's origins were changed, and the character was now purported to have grown up "near the Canadian border". Trevor's Canadian origins are something of an in-joke throughout the game, as numerous characters express anti-Canadian sentiment.
*While [[Michael De Santa]] and [[Franklin Clinton]] have the ability to kindly greet [[Pedestrians|people]] when the player presses {{Button|psright}}, Trevor on the opposite hand can only taunt people, leading them to either run away or fight him.
**Trevor also gives unique taunts to [[:Category:Gangs in GTA V|gang members]], overweight people, [[Tramps|homeless people]], hipsters, the [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD]] and the elderly.
*Trevor can trigger unique responses from pedestrians by passing by them. Most of them are of a fearful nature commenting on his looks and hygiene. He can still receive these responses even if the player controls Michael or Franklin and hangs out with Trevor, in which case Trevor can respond back with insults.
*In Trevor's trailer, there is a flag on the wall with the emblem of a special forces unit called the [[Special Airborne Unit]] with the Latin motto 'Confide Nemini'. This could have been the unit that he was training in before his discharge. Trevor can also buy a Lonewolf jacket from [[Clothing in GTA V/Suburban|Sub Urban]] which has the same logo on the back.
*Trevor gives several hints across the game that he enjoys older women. In the mission [[Hood Safari]], if Trevor uses the [[Seashark]] that the stunt woman is tied on, he says "that's why I prefer older women" when she starts to scream. In the [[Strangers and Freaks]] mission [[Vinewood Souvenirs - The Last Act]], he says to [[Al Di Napoli]] that "older women should be appreciated, like a fine cheese". [[Patricia Madrazo]], the only woman in the game apart from his [[Mrs. Philips|mother]] that he really likes and respects, is clearly older than him; she is later stated by Trevor to be 57. [[Mary-Ann Quinn]] is younger than him, but still pushing 40, and he develops an instant attraction to her; he does the same to [[Molly Schultz]] as well. On the other hand, Trevor can optionally set up [[Booty Call|booty calls]] with [[Ursula]], a random character who is much younger than he is, as well as strippers from the [[Vanilla Unicorn]] who likewise are in their twenties.
*He sometimes refers to his [[Trevor Philips Enterprises|company]]'s name as "Trevor Philips Industries" and "Trevor Philips Enterprises". Rarely, he can be heard saying "TP Conglomerate" like in the mission [[Scouting the Port]].
*Trevor's favorite radio station is [[Channel X]]. This can be assumed because during the mission [[Crystal Maze]], upon hearing [[Non-Stop-Pop FM]] or "{{WP|What a Fool Believes}}" on [[Los Santos Rock Radio]] playing, he exclaims that "the music's all wrong" and switches to Channel X which he says is "what [he's] looking for". Despite this, Los Santos Rock Radio plays on the radio in his trailer, [[Floyd Hebert|Floyd]] [[Floyd's Apartment|apartment]], and his [[Ace Liquor|liquor store/meth lab]], suggesting he somewhat likes the station, but to a lesser extent than Channel X.
**Judging from in-game animation, he also likes [[Rebel Radio]].
*[[Switch Scenes|Switching]] to Trevor from another character is a crapshoot. While Michael and Franklin will usually be involved in mundane tasks or simply driving around, Trevor is often in the midst of some [[Switch Scenes#Trevor Philips|unusual activities]], from asking a homeless man if he would like a handjob to waking up drunk in a dress at the peak of Mount Gordo. One frequent scenario is being chased by the police; this further disables switching characters until the wanted level is dealt with.
*The only time Trevor ever mentions his deceased brother [[Ryan Philips|Ryan]] is when talking to a [[Graham|man dressed as a zombie]] on [[Vinewood Boulevard (HD Universe)|Vinewood Boulevard]] in which he comments ''"You remind me of my brother Ryan."''.
*Trevor sleeps for the longest time of all the three [[protagonist]]s. When saving the game as Trevor, the game skips ahead 12 hours. Franklin sleeps for 8, while Michael sleeps for 6. One of the withdrawal symptoms of [[methamphetamine]] is sleepiness and fatigue which may explain why he sleeps the longest.
*[[The Lost MC]] bikers will always attack Trevor whenever he passes by them.
*If the player visits the in-game website PsychicShoutout and speaks with Miss Marcy as Trevor, he will hold his own penis and ask her what he is holding in his hand. She will describe his penis as small, frayed with over use and special for him, making Trevor impressed that she guessed right. Miss Marcy will then ask Trevor if his mother is proud of him, which immediately makes Trevor very angry at her for mentioning his mother, he asks what she knows about his mother and if she is alive or coming through Miss Marcy. Miss Marcy then ends the conversation.
*Sometimes, the LSPD can be heard taunting Trevor personally over the loudspeaker, shouting things like ''"Get down right now, Trevor!"''. This implies that he has had enough run-ins with the LSPD for them to know him personally.
*Ironically, despite being the most violent of the trio and the most willing to take risks, Trevor fulfills the "less" dangerous role in three of the four [[Heists in GTA V|heists]] in which he is involved. In the freighter option of [[The Merryweather Heist]], he stays behind with the [[Submersible|mini-submarine]], while Michael and Franklin fight the [[Merryweather Security|soldiers]]. In [[Blitz Play]], he stays back and provides cover again, while Franklin and Michael fight the police officers and in the obvious approach of [[The Big Score (GTA V)|The Big Score]], he flies the [[Frogger (TPE)|chopper]] to transport the gold, while Michael simulates a robbery in the [[Union Depository]] and Franklin actually steals the gold. This could be because of his potent distrust in Michael, fearing that he could be betrayed once more by his former partner if he didn't have eyes on him at all times. However, it could simply be due to him being the most-skilled pilot of the group, as he was military-trained.
**In Blitz Play, he says ''"As long as I am not on the ground when the shit goes down."''.
*Sometimes, when visiting [[Sandy Shores]] as Franklin or Michael, he can appear in his [[Trevor's Trailer|yard]] drunk, [[drinking]] alcohol and throwing [[grenades]]. If the player switches to Trevor while he is doing this, the grenades will disappear, and he will have sobered up.
**The [[Los Santos County Sheriff|police]] will not react to him, even if he kills a sheriff. Also, Trevor will not stop throwing grenades, even if the player is in the way. Interestingly, his grenades will knock cars and trucks around, as the grenades are scripted to land in the same place.
*During [[Friendships in GTA V|friendship activities]] with Franklin, Trevor reveals that if he was not expelled from school, he may have had a profession in the arts, such as a singer or an artist. He claims that he has something "very big" to tell the world but does not quite know what it is.
*Trevor has a spade tattoo on his right forearm and a dagger tattoo on his left hand in his [[Grand Theft Auto V/Artwork|artworks]], despite not having them in the final game. It is possible to buy two dagger [[Tattoos in GTA Online|tattoos]] in the game, one for each forearm. The dagger on his right forearm in the game is the one that is pictured on his left one in the artwork, and the dagger on his left forearm in the game has a dragon around it.
**One of the daggers looks exactly like the knife in ''{{Red Dead|Red Dead Redemption}}'', another game made by [[Rockstar Games]].
*Only one of Trevor's default tattoos can be removed: "R.I.P Michael". This was most likely done so that players could remove/replace the tattoo once Trevor discovered that Michael was still alive, and after Trevor found out about [[Brad Snider|Brad's]] death in [[Bury the Hatchet]], the players would place the "Betraying Scroll" tattoo on his left chest.
*Trevor's signature color is orange.
**The selection of the protagonist's signature colors may also be a reflection of the character's personalities and/or their individual story arcs. Orange is the color of amusement, madness and danger, reflecting Trevor's chaotic and eccentric lifestyle, his destructive habits, his persistent dark humor, and his general tendency to be a public menace.
*Trevor can wear Patricia Madrazo's clothes, as they are found in his [[wardrobe]]. They could be a symbol of remembrance to him of Patricia or suggest he likes wearing women's clothing, as he can also wear dresses.
*Unlike Michael and Franklin, on the [[Rockstar Games Social Club|Social Club Overview]] page, Trevor's business card amusingly states "''None of your business card''".
*Despite being scared of clowns, Trevor can get a large tattoo of a clown on his torso with some text reading "Sleep Tight!".
*It is possible to see Trevor's genitals when he is wearing a dress, making it the second instance in ''Grand Theft Auto'' history that genitalia are exposed, after [[Thomas Stubbs III]] during the [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] of [[Politics]] in {{TLAD}}. They are also briefly seen in the opening cutscene of [[Scouting the Port]], as well as being able to be seen in [[By the Book]] if the player starts the mission as Michael.
*When following him as Franklin or Michael for too long, he will headbutt either of them.
**This is in contrast to Michael and Franklin, when following them as controlling another protagonist, Michael or Franklin (depends on who is being followed) will punch whoever is being controlled.
*There are several instances where Trevor's death is foreshadowed, by others or even himself — all of which reference the alternate ending mission [[Something Sensible]] in some way.
**In one of his dialogues when talking to a random pedestrian, or when driving a vehicle and flipping off a random driver, he may say "''Why hasn't someone sensible shot you yet?".''
**During the mission [[By the Book]], if Trevor chooses not to inject [[Ferdinand Kerimov|Kerimov]] with the adrenaline shot after he loses consciousness from being tortured, a unique "Mission Failed" cutscene will play where [[Steve Haines]] will notice that Mr. K has died, and after a brief argument with Trevor, he will end the confrontation saying "''Because soon, I'll put an end to your little charade...''" before leaving. This is a hint towards Steve's view of Trevor as a liability and thus the reason he ordered Franklin to kill him, deeming him too "uncontrollable" and "insane."
**In {{O}}, during the [[Heist Setups|heist setup]] mission [[Series A - Steal Meth]], Trevor will warn the player after stealing the [[Phantom|tanker]] that the meth juice inside is really flammable, and to try and not let the [[O'Neil Brothers]] shoot it too much. He then brushes off this danger with the sarcastic remark "''But, hey, it's your flaming carcass, not mine.''".
*If Trevor meets Franklin before [[The Merryweather Heist]], Franklin will become hostile and shoot Trevor, and vice versa.
*If Trevor meets Franklin after [[The Time's Come]], Trevor will refer to Franklin as a "traitor" and threaten him.
*When viewing Trevor's "Mullet" [[Barbers in GTA Online|hairstyle]] in the mirror, his bald spot has more hair on top, similar to how he wore his hair in [[2004]].
*According to himself, he was dead for five minutes after putting an axe into a power cable.{{Ref|name=dead5minutes|ref={{Quote|Well, I'm not dead, I wasn't dead, I never have been... except for maybe five minutes when I put an axe into a power cable.|Trevor to Jimmy during [[Friendships in GTA V|friend activities]]. {{Small|({{URL|https://youtu.be/Seg7NNVQCT8?t{{=}}7329|Video}})}}}}}}
*Trevor is the least likely to change clothes automatically when switching characters, showing his little regard for hygiene or cleanliness.
*Trevor holds the position as the only GTA protagonist to have canonically killed another protagonist, as he murdered [[Johnny Klebitz]], protagonist of {{TLAD}}.
*Trevor is the protagonist who encounters most other protagonists in the series, that being four (Michael De Santa, Johnny Klebitz, Franklin Clinton, and the ''GTA Online'' Protagonist).

These quotes are the same as when the player runs out of ammunition for a weapon, or when an angry NPC is trying to attack him while he is in a vehicle.
