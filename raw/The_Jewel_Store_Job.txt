'''The Jewel Store Job''' is a [[Heists in GTA V|heist]] mission in {{V}}, undertaken by [[Michael De Santa]] and [[Franklin Clinton]].
{{toc|position=left}}
{{Clear}}
==Description==
===Preparation===
In order to perform the heist, Michael must perform a couple of side-missions ([[Heist Setups]]) to gain the necessary equipment. The preparation missions vary based on the chosen approach in [[Casing the Jewel Store]].
<tabber>
Smart=
Two preparation missions have to be performed:

*The first one, [[Bugstars Equipment]], involves stealing a [[Bugstars Burrito|van]] from the [[Bugstars]] company.
*The second, [[BZ Gas Grenades]], involves stealing [[BZ Gas|gas grenades]] from a van driving around the city.
|-|
Loud=
Only one preparation mission, [[Carbine Rifles]], has to be performed, namely stealing a set of [[Carbine Rifle|carbine rifles]] from one of the [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD's]] tactical team [[FIB Granger|vans]].
</tabber>

===Mission===
Depending on the chosen approach in Casing the Jewel Store, the start of the mission will play out in one of two ways:
<tabber>
Smart=
Driving from the garment factory in a [[Primo]], Michael will drop Franklin off at the building site, while the [[Heist Crew Members in GTA V#Drivers|driver]] and [[Heist Crew Members in GTA V#Gunmen|gunman]] park the [[Bugstars]] [[Bugstars Burrito|van]] in front of the [[Vangelico|jeweler's]]. Taking control of Franklin, the player must traverse the roofs from around the block, use the vantage point from the recon mission, and throw a can of [[BZ Gas|tear gas]] into the air vent. The gas will be released into the shop, knocking everybody inside unconscious.

Afterwards, Michael, the driver, and the gunman will enter and start ransacking the store, while Franklin goes to get the bikes. The amount of time available before the alarm starts will depend on the picked [[Heist Crew Members in GTA V#Hackers|hacker]], but since the gold medal requires the player to steal the jewelry within fifty seconds, any of them will do (in the patched version; in the original console release, [[Rickie Lukens]] could only hold the alarm off for thirty seconds, while it takes a minimum of 43 seconds in the smart approach to clear all the jewel cases). A poor gunman ([[Norm Richards]]) will drop some of the jewelry, reducing the take to approximately $4.5 million.
|-|
Loud=
{{Quote|Ladies and gentlemen, this is your moment! Please, don't make me ruin all the great work your plastic surgeons have been doing. On the floor, now!|Michael De Santa, Loud approach}}
Franklin drives Michael, the driver and the gunman to the jeweler's in a [[Burrito|van]]. Donning helmets, they will enter and tell the hostages to get down. While the gunman holds the hostages, Michael and the driver start ransacking the store, while Franklin waits outside with the bikes. The store manager will try to intervene and a good gunman ([[Gustavo Mota|Gus Mota]] or [[Patrick McReary|Packie McReary]]) will handle it, but if a bad one ([[Norm Richards]]) is chosen, he will do poorly with crowd control and Michael will have to kill the manager, or they'll sound the alarm and fail the mission. The amount of time available before the alarm starts will depend on the selected hacker, but since the gold medal requires the player to steal the jewelry within fifty seconds, any of them will do (in the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|enhanced edition]]; in the original version Rickie Lukens can only hold the alarm off for thirty seconds, while it takes a minimum of 49 seconds in the loud approach to clear all the jewel cases). A poor driver ([[Karim Denz]]) will drop some of the jewelry, reducing the take to approximately $4.5 million.

It can be noted that the [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino Heist]] DLC mission [[Casino Heist - The Big Con]] confirms that this is not the [[canon]] approach to The Jewel Store Job.
</tabber>

===Escape===
Both approaches lead to the same escape. Once the robbery has been committed then causing the player get 4 stars [[Wanted Level in GTA V|Wanted Level]]. Franklin escapes on a bike with the driver and gunman. The motorcycle depends on the driver, but won't make much of a difference. Karim Denz, a poor driver, will send panicky and unhelpful radio traffic, rather than the calm and professional directions from pro [[Eddie Toh]], but this really doesn't affect anything either. Follow the crew, evading the [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|cops]], and drive into the tunnel to access the sewers. If a low-skilled gunman is chosen, then he, along with his bag of jewels, will crash and fall off his bike before entering the tunnel. If the player picks up the bag, the take will not change, but if not; a significant amount of the takings are lost. There is a casualty expense for the death of the gunman, regardless. If a high-skilled gunman is chosen, none of this happens.

Using the Los Santos sewer network to evade the police, the crew eventually ends up at the [[Los Santos Storm Drain (HD Universe)|Los Santos River]] into a pack of awaiting police cars. In the meantime, Michael and the hacker went to get the [[Benson|delivery truck]] and use it to forcibly push the cops out of the way.

Once the police attention has been lost, the bikes are loaded onto the truck and are driven to Lester's lockup between [[Davis]] and [[Rancho]] where the crew splits up. The maximum take for the mission is [[Money|$]]4,946,153 if the player smashes all twenty displays and chooses a good gunman (for either approach) or a good driver (for the loud approach). $2,500,000 will then go to [[Martin Madrazo]], while the rest gets split between the crew members. Note that for this first heist, Franklin receives a fixed percentage as a hired gunman/driver, rather than splitting the take equally with Michael.

After completing the mission, a [[Switch Scenes|switch scene]] with Franklin shows that [[Tanisha Jackson|Tanisha]] was none too thrilled with Franklin's newfound wealth while he is relaxing at the [[Vanilla Unicorn]]. Franklin wanted to take her out on a date, which she refuses as Franklin told her that he had to leave and that he hopes to see her soon.

==Maximum Revenue==
For the largest possible take, choose the smart approach and hire [[Karim Denz]] as the driver, [[Packie McReary]] as the gunman and [[Rickie Lukens]] as the hacker. This will net Michael $1,223,073 and Franklin $293,539.

'''Maximum gross take''': $4,946,153

'''Martin Madrazo's payoff''': -$2,500,000

'''Maximum net take''': $2,446,153

'''Lester's cut (14%)''': -$342,462

'''Crew cut (24%)''': -$587,077

'''Franklin's cut (12%)''': $293,539

'''Michael's profit (50%)''': $1,223,073

===Gold Medal Objectives===
====Loud and Smart====
*Quick Grab - Steal the jewelry within 50 seconds.
**Note that this is not the same as Clean Sweep: all that is required is that you grab the minimum $3.3 million in jewelry and leave the store within 50 seconds.
**Move as fast as possible with either approach.
**In the loud approach, hire a good gunman (Gus or Packie), since otherwise dealing with the store manager will cost Michael precious time.
**Using [[First Person View|first person view]] helps as there is no delay between the character moving and the animation of breaking each of the glass cabinets ([[PlayStation 4|PS4]], [[Xbox One]] and [[Personal Computer|PC]] only).
**Shooting the glass will remove the breaking animation, making it a bit faster.
**Using Michael's [[Special Ability|special ability]] before breaking the cases will also save some time.
**If this is proving difficult, use Rickie Lukens as the hacker, since the alarm will go off after 50 seconds, autofailing the mission and allowing a checkpoint retry.
*Protégé Protected - Protect Franklin during the LS River chase.
**It can simply be done without failing the mission; make sure Franklin is not killed by the LSPD during the chase.
*Clean Sweep - Steal the jewelry from all 20 glass cabinets.
**Move fast and use Patrick McReary or Gustavo Mota as the gunman, and (for the loud approach only) [[Eddie Toh]] as the driver.

==Oversights==
*At the time of the robbery, people present in the store include the [[Jewelry Store Clerk|clerk]], the manager, the security guard, a man speaking with the clerk and a couple close to the door. In the smart approach, after everyone collapses, the clerk and the woman's places will be interchanged, as the woman will be behind the counter instead of the clerk. 
*When [[Rickie Luckens|Rickie]] says ''"Uhh, bros? Just to warn you, we're not the only people waiting for you in the LS River."'', the subtitles say ''"Uhh. Bros, just to warn you, there might be some cops waiting for you in the LS River."'' instead. This was fixed in the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|enhanced version]].
*During the escape sequence, once in control of the [[Benson]], the player can immediately exit the truck and pull the [[Heist Crew Members in GTA V#Hackers|hacker]] out of the truck via the passenger side. Provided the player does not kill the hacker, they are free to continue the pursuit and the hacker's voice lines will still play as if they were still in the truck for the remainder of the mission.
**Upon loading the motorcycles into the truck, the hacker will reappear as normal.
*If the Benson is damaged and the rear door is lowered after loading the motorcycles into the truck, the motorcycles will not appear in the rear of the truck.

==Trivia==
===Beta===
*The mission first appeared in a scene in the [[Grand Theft Auto V/Trailers|first trailer]]. It was later depicted in an [[Grand Theft Auto V/Artwork|artwork]] piece almost a year later. It was also the first confirmed mission of {{V}}.
**Also, in the artwork of [[Los Santos River (HD Universe)|LS Storm Drain]], while hard to see, there's a [[Bravado]] [[Police Cruiser (Buffalo)|Police Cruiser]] and a [[Vapid]] [[Police Cruiser]], which was later replaced by the [[Police Cruiser (interceptor)|Interceptor]].
*In the version of the mission seen in the first trailer, [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] is seen completing the job with [[Gustavo Mota]], [[Eddie Toh]], and another unknown [[Heist Crew Members in GTA V|crew]] member. It is possible that this crew member is meant to represent [[Patrick McReary|Packie]] due to their similar hairstyles.
**This, as well as a piece of [[:File:Artwork-Lester-GTAV.jpg|artwork]] inside Darnell Bros. showing four crew members' wrap sheets on the planning board, suggests the mission would originally have four crew members.
**Also, the crew uses the smart approach equipment: the [[Bugstars]] [[Bugstars Burrito|van]], uniform, and gas masks.
**In the same trailer, the [[Gas Mask|gas masks]] were yellow and covered only the mouth.
*Also in the beta, balaclavas were used in the loud approach, but motorbike helmets were used in the final version.

===Influence===
*The mission, especially the loud method, seems to be loosely inspired in the opening scene of the 1987 film ''{{WP|Beverly Hills Cop II|Beverly Hills Cop 2}}'', where a crew of robbers hold a jewelry store, doing the same motions as breaking the glass panels and being timed to pick up the jewels.
*The escape route seems to be loosely based on the 2003 film ''{{WP|The Italian Job (2003 film)|The Italian Job}}'', as the crew escape through the sewers in {{WP|Mini Cooper}}s, chased by bikers and exiting through the flood control.
*The use of [[BZ Gas|BZ gas]] and the pest control van could be used in reference to an episode of ''{{WP|NCIS (TV Series)}}'', "{{WC|ncis|Sharif Returns (episode)|Sharif Returns}}", where the gas was used in a more lethal manner, and pest control vans were mentioned in conjunction during a newscast.

===General===
*New ''{{Red Dead|Red Dead Redemption}}'' copies are sold with ''GTA V'' promotional cards, with an image of the mission occurring.
*The screenshot on the [[Rockstar Games Social Club]] shows [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] wearing his default gray suit with gloves and a [[balaclava]] for the loud option.
*On the corkboard in the gameplay trailer, a to-do list showed three out of the five tasks were complete, those completed tasks being: evaluating the security, photographing the interior and deciding the approach for the heist.
*[[Lester Crest|Lester]] mentions the {{GTAOP}} as a possible contact they could use for the [[Heists in GTA V|heist]], but ultimately decides against it due to their unpredictable nature making them a bad fit for the operation.
*It takes, at minimum, six seconds longer to clear the jewel cases in the loud approach compared to the smart approach, because of the manager's attempt at heroism.
*Killing any hostages with a skilled gunman will cause the gunman to berate Michael for killing the hostages. This will not fail the mission, however.
*If Michael stays in the [[Vangelico|store]] after the timer runs out, a unique cutscene will play, where the [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|police]] arrive and arrest the crew members. Michael is seen getting handcuffed and at the end escorted to a police vehicle. This will also fail the mission.{{ref|name=uniquecutscene|type=youtube|url={{YT|iz4s3SWb9GE|GTA 5 PS3 - Mission #12 - The Jewel Store Job - Alternative Cutscene (Loud)}}|date = 2026-01-31}}
*The [[Albert Stalley|traffic warden]] cannot be shot and killed, as he reappears in the first [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] of [[Mr. Philips]]. However, he can be killed if the player runs him over.
*If [[Norm Richards|Norm]] and [[Karim Denz|Karim]] are chosen as part of the crew, after Norm crashes into the entrance of the tunnel, Karim will stop halfway into the underground route trying to get his bearings.
*If [[Eddie Toh]] is chosen as the driver, he will call the [[Sanchez]] "the best bikes for the terrain" in the tunnel. This is correct, as the Sanchez and its [[Sanchez (livery)|livery variation]] can easily handle off-road terrain.
*If Karim Denz is chosen as the driver, the [[Bati 801RR]] used for the getaway features a unique engine sound. Selecting him is one of the very few ways to get a Bati 801RR in story mode, especially in the enhanced version ([[Xbox One]], [[PlayStation 4|PS4]], and [[Personal Computer|PC]]), due to it being absent from traffic.
**Moreover, in the original version ([[Xbox 360]] and [[PlayStation 3|PS3]]), replaying this mission and selecting Karim Denz as the driver would slightly improve the probability of the Bati 801RR spawning in traffic.
*If the loud approach is chosen, Michael's [[Carbine Rifle]] will retain approximately 2,000 cartridges after the mission. If the smart approach is chosen, Michael's [[Assault Rifle]] will retain approximately the same amount. The extra ammo for both will disappear after the mission [[Three's Company]] is completed.
*This is one of the few missions where it is impossible to win a bronze medal, since the Protege Protected objective must be completed or the mission fails.
*This mission is one of the few missions other than the [[Lester's Assassinations|assassination missions]] that affects the stock market. As soon as the mission is finished, [[Vangelico]] stock falls, but it rebounds after [[Minor Turbulence]].

===Smart===
*If [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] has [[grenades]], Michael will notice the grenades and warn Franklin just before he reaches the vantage point not to throw a grenade by mistake.
*If [[Eddie Toh]] is selected as the getaway driver, [[Gustavo Mota]] or [[Patrick McReary]] is selected as the gunman, and [[Paige Harris]] is selected as the hacker, Michael will complain to Franklin about their high cuts on the way to the construction site.
*Michael and his team are with the [[Bugstars Burrito]], unlike in the loud approach, where they are seen with a different black [[Burrito]].
*After loading the motorcycles into the Benson, the radio will be set to [[Radio Mirror Park]]. One of the following three songs may be playing: "Little White Lie" by Living Days, "Shooting Holes" by [[Twin Shadow]] or "Boogie in Zero Gravity" by {{WP|Black Strobe}}.
*If [[Casino Heist - The Big Con|The Big Con]] with Bugstar disguise approach is chosen during [[The Diamond Casino Heist]], [[Lester Crest|Lester]] will mention that he's "done something similar to this before, a jewel store", confirming that the smart approach is the [[canon]] choice for this mission.
