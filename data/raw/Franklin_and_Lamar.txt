'''Franklin and Lamar''' is a mission in {{V}}, given to [[Franklin Clinton]] by car dealer [[Simeon Yetarian]]. Fellow protagonist [[Michael De Santa]] also makes an appearance in the opening cutscene of the mission.

==Description==
{{Dialogue|p1=Lamar|d1=You the one all pumped up on doing this lick, nigga. I'm getting my money in the hood, I'm straight, fool. I'm cool.|p2=Franklin|d2=You cool? Cool what? Slinging dope and throwing up gang signs?|quotedetail=[[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] and [[Lamar Davis|Lamar]] at the beginning of the mission.|sound=FranklinandLamar-GTAV-QuoteGTAWiki.ogg}}
Nine years after the events of the [[Prologue (GTA V)|Prologue]], [[Michael De Santa]] is having a conversation with his therapist, [[Isiah Friedlander|Dr. Isiah Friedlander]]. Michael is growing increasingly unhappy at the fact that he is going through a midlife crisis, beginning to release a small amount of stress by shouting at Dr. Friedlander that the perfect life he dreamt of is disjointed, thus ending their brief session for the day.

Michael heads outside and sits on a bench overlooking [[Del Perro Beach]]. This brings him into a chance encounter with [[Lamar Davis]] and [[Franklin Clinton]], who he reluctantly redirects to their repossession location of [[Bertolt Beach House]]. The story then turns to focus on Franklin as he anxiously questions Lamar's motives for proceeding with the repossession, as he doesn't see it as legitimate work. As they sneak onto the property, Franklin is given a choice between reposessing a [[9F Cabrio]] or a [[Rapid GT]]. If the player stays at the house after entering the vehicle, the car owner will pull Franklin from the car and start attacking him.

After acquiring the vehicles, Franklin and Lamar engage in a high-speed chase throughout different parts of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] using the resposessed vehicles, with Lamar egging on Franklin to push the cars to their limit. If Franklin manages to pass Lamar during the race, he will react negatively, saying "How the fuck did you get ahead of me?". In the beginning of the chase, Franklin urges Lamar to drive carefully since Simeon cuts the cost from the employee's paychecks if the repossessed car is damaged. In return, Lamar suggests Franklin to work for [[JB Bradshaw]]'s [[LSPD Auto Impound|tow business]] if he needs money, to which Franklin refuses, as he does not want to be instrumental for JB and [[Tonya Wiggins|Tonya]] "smoking crack in peace". This dialogue ironically foreshadows Franklin's free help for JB and Tonya to tow vehicles later on in five different occasions starting with the [[Strangers and Freaks|mission]] [[Pulling Favors]], which are caused by JB's drug addiction.

Franklin and Lamar end their chase at the [[Union Depository]] car park and exchange repartee with each other. If Franklin's car has sustained heavy damage by this point, Lamar will comment on the state of his vehicle. After hearing [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD]] sirens in the distance, the pair drive away in different directions to lose the unwanted police attention. Once the two-star [[Wanted Level in GTA V|wanted level]] is lost, Franklin meets Lamar at the back of the [[Premium Deluxe Motorsport]] dealership, where they witness their boss and owner of the car dealership, [[Simeon Yetarian]], berating a young man, [[Jimmy De Santa]], for making racist comments about him, which in actuality is part of Simeon's scheme to force Jimmy into purchasing  a [[BeeJay XL]], something that Lamar himself points out to Franklin. After Franklin and Lamar decide to leave the dealership, Simeon will continue to encourage Jimmy to purchase the car, with Jimmy getting in the car and starting it. When the conversation ends, Simeon tells Franklin to go away.

Both Lamar and Franklin leave the dealership and head to Franklin's [[Clinton Residence|house]], where Lamar mocks Franklin's haircut after he's been denied access to Franklin's home. The mission concludes as Franklin walks around the house, which also leads to the brief introduction of his aunt, [[Denise Clinton]], and the basics of the game.

==Rewards==
The reward for completing the mission is $250 and the [[Welcome to Los Santos (Achievement)|Welcome to Los Santos]] [[Achievements and Trophies in GTA V|Achievement/Trophy]].

Any bonus content, such as returning player bonuses ([[Xbox One|X1]]/[[PlayStation 4|PS4]]/[[Personal Computer|PC]] only), DLC weapons ([[Xbox 360|X360]]/[[PlayStation 3|PS3]] only), [[Interaction Menu]], [[Rockstar Games Social Club|Social Club]] content, or [[Grand Theft Auto V/Editions|Special/Collector's Edition]] and pre-order bonuses, will also be unlocked once the player leaves the safehouse.

[[Pistol]], [[Micro SMG]], [[SMG]], [[Assault SMG]] (Rockstar Social Club bonus), [[Assault Rifle]], [[Knife]], [[Grenades]], and all five types of [[Body Armor]], are unlocked at [[Ammu-Nation (HD Universe)|Ammu-Nation]], including the [[Ammu-Nation Shooting Ranges|shooting range]] activity. The player can also go to [[Los Santos Customs]] and [[Beeker's Garage]], [[Car Washes]], [[Movies (GTA V)|Movie Theatres]], [[Barbers#HD Universe|Barber Shops]] (as suggested by Lamar after the mission), [[Vanilla Unicorn|Strip Club]], [[Cable Car]]s, and any fairground ride ([[Ferris Whale]] and [[Leviathan (roller coaster)|Leviathan]]), perform [[Stunt Jumps in GTA V|Stunt Jumps]], find [[Peyote Plants in GTA V|Peyote Plants]] (Enhanced Version), complete [[Taxi Jobs in GTA V|Taxi Fares]], use in-game [[Internet in GTA V|Internet]] (including [[Bawsaq.com|Stock]] [[Liberty City National Exchange|Markets]]), [[Robberies#Grand Theft Auto V and Grand Theft Auto Online|convenience stores]] are open from now on, as well as use the [[Atomic Blimp]] or complete [[Stunt Plane Time Trials]] (both are pre-order bonuses, also available in Enhanced Version).

==Gameplay Conditions==
*In the original version of {{V|l=n}}, the [[Radio Stations in GTA V|radio]] is automatically tuned into [[West Coast Classics]] and the song playing when Franklin begins racing with Lamar is always "Appetite for Destruction" by {{WP|N.W.A}} In the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|Enhanced]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V and Online: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S Editions|Expanded & Enhanced]] editions, the radio is tuned into [[Radio Los Santos]], and the song playing instead is always "Bad News" by {{WP|Action Bronson}} and {{WP|Danny Brown}}.
*In the enhanced version, a randomly colored [[Alpha]] can be seen in the parking area the red [[Carbonizzare]] enters.
*Because of the scripted nature of the race, Lamar's car is both invulnerable and immovable. If Franklin manages to catch up to Lamar, he will be completely unable to affect Lamar's car in any way.
*Franklin cannot use his special ability until the tutorial prompt tells the player how to activate it.
*As with other driving-focused missions in {{V|l=n}}, much of the traffic over the course of the race portion of the mission is scripted:
**A [[Youga]], a [[Flatbed]], a [[Manana]] and four Intruders along the [[Great Ocean Highway]].
**A Manana and three Intruders on the [[Bay City Incline]].
**An Intruder and two Mananas on [[Bay City Avenue]].
**A [[PCJ 600]], a [[Burrito]], nine Mananas and eleven Intruders along [[North Rockford Drive]].
**Two Burritos, three Mananas, five Intruders and a group of cyclists along [[Marathon Avenue]].
**A Manana driving down [[Heritage Way]].
**An Intruder and a backing up [[Benson]] on [[Red Desert Avenue]].
**A [[Packer]], a Youga, two PCJ 600s, four Intruders and five Mananas on or near [[South Rockford Drive]].
**Three Mananas and three Intruders on [[Prosperity Street]].
**Two Bensons, six Mananas and six Intruders on [[Palomino Avenue]].
**An Intruder travelling along [[Lindsay Circus]].
**Two Mananas and six Intruders along [[Calais Avenue]].
**A [[Bus]], a PCJ 600, two Intruders and three Mananas on [[Vespucci Boulevard]].
**A Burrito and four Intruders within the adjacent parking lot, two Intruders on [[San Andreas Avenue]].
**A sole Intruder on [[Las Lagunas Boulevard]].
**A Benson on [[Alta Street]].

==Oversights==
*After the [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] turns into gameplay, Michael will no longer be sitting on the bench.
*When Franklin grabs Lamar's arm, only one car can immediately be seen parked behind them. In the next shot, however, many more can be seen.
*If Franklin is ahead of Lamar inside the [[Backlot City|movie studio]], and avoids the aliens, Lamar may occasionally hit the aliens himself, preventing the player from obtaining the "We Come in Peace" gold medal objective despite not hitting the aliens themselves.

==Trivia==
*The ending cutscene of the mission, where Franklin parts with Lamar, who in return mocks Franklin's haircut and love life, would later grow to become a popular internet meme in the early 2020s.
**In 2021, [[Shawn Fonteno]] and [[Slink Johnson]] reprised their roles as Franklin and Lamar respectively to {{URL|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}cU0oHq9Y6Ws|re-enact}} the cutscene at the end of the mission.
**The ending cutscene would also be referenced at the end of the mission [[Short Trip - Fire It Up]], released with [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update in [[2021]]. In the scene, Lamar berates Franklin about his poor family harmony in a similar delivery and tone, with some lines of dialogue directly shared from the original cutscene.
*This mission shares similarities to the {{SA}} mission [[Test Drive]], in which [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] and [[Cesar Vialpando|Cesar]] steal [[Sultan (3D Universe)|two]] [[Elegy (3D Universe)|cars]] from [[Otto's Autos]] and must escape from the police.
*This mission also shares similarities to the {{SA}} mission [[Sweet & Kendl]], in which [[Ryder]] tells CJ to get a haircut as it's embarrassing to be seen with him.
